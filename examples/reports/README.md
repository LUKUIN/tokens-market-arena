# Sample Match Reports

Every finished match produces one self-contained HTML file (no external dependencies, double-click
to open). Four representative ones are included here:

> **Format note**: `01`–`03` were produced by an early engine (v97) and **do not contain** the
> random seed, the cost attribution section, the 10-column trade list, or the per-loan record.
> `04` is a complete report from the current engine — open that one to see what today's report
> looks like.

## 01 · The only match that hit the target · qwen3.8-flash · +106.38%

```
Final equity   2,063,829.17    target 2,000,000    rating A
P&L            +1,063,829.17 (+106.38%)
Duration       15 min 45 s
Max drawdown   21.86%    Sharpe 2.29    14 trades    win rate 50%
```

**Why it won**: the win rate was only 50%, but the **profit/loss ratio was 3:1**.

The turning point was tick 121 — it accepted a 230k loss (closing the AURA short at −197,567 and
the TIDE short at −32,547) and simultaneously flipped into a 6,000-lot LUMEN long. LUMEN then ran
from 0.9604 to 1.0610 (+20.57%) and was scaled out in three batches for +1.09M.

**Willing to cut, and willing to add.**

## 04 · Full example in the current format · generated by the current engine

The point of this file is not its P&L but that it contains **every section the report has today**:
random seed and how to reproduce, the concrete difficulty parameters (gap probability / liquidity
depth), five-part cost attribution (gross P&L / slippage / spread / commission / overnight
interest), the 10-column trade list, per-loan records with target uplift, the scratchpad, and the
event feed.

## 02 · A typical failure · WorkBuddy-Agent · −76.95%

```
Final equity   230,483.51    target 2,000,000    rating D
P&L            −769,516.49 (−76.95%)
```

Both the operations and the event feed are in the report. It shows the most common way to die in
this game:

**Not by getting the direction wrong, but by dying to cost.** Commission plus overnight interest
accounted for a large share of the total loss, and repeatedly going in and out over 45 minutes
piles the cost up to something unbearable.

## 03 · A small loss · GLM-5.3-Flash · −9.10%

```
Final equity   909,030.39    target 2,000,000    rating D
P&L            −90,969.61 (−9.10%)
Duration       38 min 51 s
Max drawdown   14.06%    Sharpe −0.89    11 trades    win rate 36.4%
```

small amount earns you nothing extra.

## How to read a report

| Section | What to look at |
|---|---|
| **Equity curve** | Green dashed line is starting capital, gold is the target, red is zero. Crossing red marks the match as blown out |
| **Key metrics** | Black Swan frequency / liquidity tier — this match's difficulty |
| **Loan ledger** | How much borrowed, repaid, interest, and cash before/after each repayment |
| **Trade list** | Every trade, untruncated |
| **Event feed** | Every event, including gaps, liquidity droughts and policy events |
| **Scratchpad** | The reasoning the AI wrote down during the match (the Human edition has no such section) |
